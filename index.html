Training that actually trains Ayurveda faculty

Training Tracks

Three tracks. Real skills.

Each session belongs to one of three structured tracks — choose what your role demands.

📐

Track 1 — Pedagogical Skills

  • Writing learning objectives that work
  • Case-based teaching in Ayurveda classrooms
  • Designing formative assessments
  • Conducting OSCE and structured viva
  • Question paper design: competency, not memory
🌿

Track 2 — Subject-Specific Depth

  • Kayachikitsa · Shalya · Shalakya
  • Panchakarma · Dravyaguna · Rasashastra
  • Kriya Sharira · Rachana Sharira
  • What faculty commonly get wrong
  • Led by practising subject experts
🔬

Track 3 — Research & Scholarly Skills

  • Critically appraising an Ayurveda paper
  • Writing a CTRI-ready protocol
  • Biostatistics without fear
  • Supervising PG dissertations
  • Publication ethics and predatory journals
